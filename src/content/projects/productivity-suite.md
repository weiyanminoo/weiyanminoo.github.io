- Captures ideas and random thoughts in one quick idea dump, then builds on them with suggestions and follow-ups.
- Takes a task with its scope, deadline and importance, and works out the best time to do it.
- Adds events to the calendar without opening the app.
- Reprioritises anything unfinished and sets its next deadline.
