## Too many tabs open

I always have a lot of ideas and random thoughts. They show up whenever they like, usually at the worst time, and keeping track of them is hard. What helps is writing them down quickly. Once a thought is out of my head, I don't have to keep holding on to it, and that clears a lot of mental load.

## Decision fatigue

Since the start of 2025, I've been juggling school, work and my own projects every day. That comes with a lot of small decisions. What do I do first? Can this wait until tomorrow? Is this more important than that?

None of them are hard on their own. But they pile up, and by the time a decision comes along that needs real thought, I've already spent a lot of my energy on the small ones.

So I wanted to hand the everyday decisions to a personal agent. It takes care of what to do and when, and I get to focus on the ones that need proper critical thinking.

## What's in it

- **An idea dump.** One place to throw every idea and random thought, as fast as I can type it. My agent then builds on each idea and comes back with suggestions or follow-ups.
- **A planner that plans for me.** I tell it what I need to do, with a rough scope, a deadline and how important it is. It figures out the best time for me to do it.
- **A quick calendar.** I can add things to my calendar in a few seconds, without opening the calendar app.
- **A plan B for my plan A.** If I don't finish something by the day it was meant for, it reprioritises the task and sets a new deadline. It's a lot more forgiving than I am.

I'm building it for one user, me. If it means I spend less time deciding what to do and more time actually doing it, that's a win.
